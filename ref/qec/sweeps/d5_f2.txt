# d = 5 float64 baselines 2 (grumpy-calf), 2026-10-26: narrow / deep nets as warm-start sources for small cores.
# Why: the 256x3-derived HGQ core at 3.58M EBOPs (~500k LUT) does not route at 161 MHz on the VU9P (q5), and
# shrinking 256x3 by EBOPs pressure costs accuracy fast (1.21 at 1.9M). Depth helped at d = 5 (2-layer nets
# plateaued at 1.38-1.42, 3-layer at 0.93-0.97), so try fewer weights with the same or more depth.
